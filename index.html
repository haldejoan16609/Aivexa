THE BFSI PROBLEM

High-friction workflows slow teams down.

Customer cases and operational requests can span email, documents, policies, case systems and internal knowledge. Employees repeatedly search, interpret, verify and prepare actions—creating coordination overhead while accuracy and human judgment remain essential.

01

Fragmented information

Relevant evidence can live across multiple approved sources and systems.

02

Manual coordination

Research, analysis, drafting and review are often stitched together by people.

03

Need for control

Financial-services workflows require traceability, permissions and human oversight.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Specialized agents, coordinated by one workflow.

The prototype separates responsibilities so research, reasoning and review can be evaluated independently. Enterprise actions can remain gated by authorization and human approval.

01

Orchestrator

Breaks the business goal into tasks and routes them to the right agent.

02

03

Review + Approval

Checks output quality and escalates consequential actions to an authorized human.
